## Jon's rulings of October 21, 2026, on stage c3a's open questions

- **WM.65 (open question 3).** A place event type's record in `definition.json` gains the key `place_kind: move | act`, for drawn and explicit place event types alike, so that the corpus and the Rust runtime read the kind rather than infer it. The key is part of the rule-set identity; the loader checks that it agrees with the record (a move has its add on a location fluent, an act its location literal), and a disagreement is a `DefinitionError`. A place event type without dynamics has no key. This moves the identity of the chain example's world once, and of every hand fixture once (the hand world has three place event types with dynamics, which write the key); both are recorded below.
- **WM.66 (open question 8).** A literal's achievability counts every effect of the world, the place event types' included. The checker (`check_dynamics`) and the checks of explicit entries read the place event types' effects, so that an entity entry whose literal only an act's effect can make true is accepted. In generation, the widest pool the draw order allows is used without making any world wrong (a literal judged achievable must be achievable); the order chosen, and its reasons, are choice 8 below, and every world without place dynamics is unchanged.

Jon's earlier rulings that apply: WM.62 (`data/world/tiny.yaml`'s `place_event_types.constraint_families` becomes `{patient: 1, cross: 1, key_lock: 1}`, the agent family at 0, so that every tiny place requirement reads the place; `tiny_full.yaml` copies the change) and WM.63 (the location settings go into `tiny_full.yaml` and `default_full.yaml`; the code's default `pair_fluents.locations` stays 0 until c4; the corpus keeps refusing every world with pair fluents). Where the c3b entry says "the default world" for locations, read `default_full`. Conditions on a place's features stay unsupported (c3a's open question 4): a drawn condition of a place event type's effect reads the agent, or `SOMEOF` of the place.

### Reading of the scope

- **Every draw of the stage happens only in a world with location fluents**, and the stream `world:place_dynamics` comes after every existing stream, so every world without location fluents draws as in stage c3a. The tiny world differs through WM.62's configuration change alone, which changes the draws of `world:place_event_types`; the default world, the default corpus, and `tiny_pairs` do not move.
- **"Drawn as for the other event types."** A place event type's further literals and effects follow the procedures of `_Dynamics`: the number of effects from `event_types.effects.count`, each a Boolean effect of the agent replaced by a numeric one at `numeric_share`; the number of literals from `event_types.preconditions.literals`, each a Boolean literal of the agent (an enabling one at `enabled_share`), replaced by a numeric literal at `numeric_share` and by a pair literal at `pair_share`; a condition at `conditional_share` with `condition_literals` literals of the kinds in `condition_kinds`, on the agent (feature, fluent, numeric) or a pair literal; every rule of stage c3a's checker (no contradiction, no empty effect, every literal achievable, no condition on what the precondition fixes or names, no condition on the fluent the effect sets, no literal twice) a redraw rather than an error. Every draw comes from `world:place_dynamics`, in label order; the other event types' streams are not read, so the entity event types' draws are unchanged by the place dynamics.
- **The pair literals a place event type can draw.** By stage c3a's rules (`location_literal_problem`, and the checker's rule that a `pair` literal on a location fluent names the entry's own location fluent), a place event type's `pair` literal on a location fluent is its location literal, already present, and `SAME` on a location fluent is refused between an entity and a place; so the drawable pair literals are `SOME` and `SOMEOF` of the agent on the pair fluents between entities and `SOMEOF` of the place on the event type's location fluent, each positive or negated.
- **The quota and the share.** `SAME.PAIRFL.k(agent, patient)` on a location fluent joins what the share can draw for a two-place event type (the `same` weight of `literal_kinds`, over the functional pair fluents between entities that are not symmetric and the location fluents), from a part of `world:pair_preconditions` of its own, so that a world without location fluents draws from the stream as before. The quota applies to two-place event types that lack the literal, have an able binding, and whose other literals leave the slot free, and counts what it adds; a world with fewer qualifying event types than the quota records the shortfall.
- **WM.66 in generation.** The entity event types are drawn before the place dynamics (stage c3a's order), so their pool holds the initial values, the entity effects, and the effects of the event file's place entries (fixed before any draw); the place dynamics' pool holds every effect of the world; the redraws of never-legal entity event types and every checker read every effect. The reasons are choice 8 below.
- **Tests of `tiny_full` are in the fast tier; tests of `default_full` that need a default-scale run are in the slow tier.**

## Engineering choices made while building

Numbered for `docs/DECISIONS.md` (WM.E307 to WM.E318). Each is Claude Code's choice inside the stage's scope.

1. **The stream.** `world:place_dynamics` is created after `world:habitats`, the last stream, and every draw of the stage happens only in a world with location fluents, so every world without them draws as in stage c3a apart from the stream's seed in `config.yaml`. The place dynamics are drawn from the stream itself, in label order; a never-legal place event type draws again from its own part, `world:place_dynamics:<label>`; the quota `same_rules` and the `SAME` literals on location fluents draw from parts of `world:pair_preconditions` of their own (`world:pair_preconditions:same_rules`, `world:pair_preconditions:locations`), and a redraw of an entity event type uses its own part for both.
2. **The drawn dynamics** (`draw_place_dynamics` in `place_dynamics.py`, the class `_PlaceDrawer`). Every place event type without an event-file entry draws, after the entity event types and the explicit entries, in label order: its kind by one draw against `move_share`; its location fluent uniformly among the world's; its effects, their number from `event_types.effects.count`, each a Boolean effect on the agent replaced by a numeric one at `effects.numeric_share`, a slot taken drawn again; its further precondition literals, their number from `preconditions.literals`, each a Boolean literal on the agent (an enabling one at `enabled_share`, over the values every effect of the world produces), achievable, in a free slot, making no Boolean effect of the event type empty, replaced by a numeric threshold at `numeric_share` and by a pair literal at `pair_share`; and a condition on each further effect at `conditional_share`, its literals' number from `condition_literals`, their kinds by `condition_kinds` among the kinds the event type can take (a static feature, a Boolean fluent, or a numeric threshold of the agent, or a pair literal), achievable, no symbol twice, not reading what the precondition fixes or names, not the fluent the effect sets, and with some able binding (an agent able at some place) satisfying the static literals, a condition drawn again up to the generator's limit and then dropped. Every rule the stage c3a checker holds is a redraw here. The compiled `move` literal or the `act` literal comes last in the precondition, as the compiled literals of the entity event types do, and a move's add first among its effects. The counts of the draws go to `Places.dynamics_stats` and to `world_stats.yaml` under `places.dynamics`.
3. **A move's further effects.** A move draws its effects by `event_types.effects.count` with its add counting as the first: a count of 1 gives the add alone, a count of 2 the add and one further effect. Reason: the specification calls the add "the effect" of a move and says it "may have further effects"; counting the add makes a move change as much as an act or a two-place event type on average (1.3 effects under the default weights), where drawing the count on top of the add would make moves the event types that change most.
4. **The pair literals a place event type draws** (`random_place_pair_literal`). `SOME` and `SOMEOF` of the agent on the pair fluents between entities, and `SOMEOF` of the place on the event type's own location fluent: the pair fluent uniformly over that pool, then the form (for a pair fluent between entities) and the polarity, each uniformly. The kind is not drawn: by stage c3a's rules a place event type's `pair` literal on its location fluent is its location literal, already present (and one on another location fluent is refused by the checker), and `SAME` is never read between an entity and a place, so `some` is the one kind a place event type can take; it draws pair literals, in preconditions and in conditions, only when `literal_kinds.some` is positive. A candidate implied by or contradicting another pair literal of the precondition, the location literal included, is drawn again (Jon's ruling 2 on stage c2b), so `SOMEOF` of the place is read positively, in moves alone: in an act the agent's presence entails it, and a move to an empty place would make the compiled literal redundant.
5. **Never-legal place event types** are drawn again in the redraw rounds of `define` as the entity event types are (`redraw_place_preconditions`): the further literals are drawn again from `world:place_dynamics:<label>`, with every effect of the world in the pool and the conditions' reads and names left free; the kind, the location fluent, the location literal, and the effects with their conditions stay; an explicit place entry is never drawn again. `define` splits the never-legal labels between the entity and the place event types and counts both under `precondition_redraws`.
6. **`SAME` on a location fluent at the share.** `random_pair_literal` takes a `location_rng`, the part `world:pair_preconditions:locations`, created only when the world has location fluents: for a two-place event type the `same` kind is drawable when the world has a location fluent or a functional pair fluent between entities that is not symmetric, and when `same` is drawn the part draws uniformly over both pools; a draw that falls on a location fluent gives `SAME.PAIRFL.k(agent, patient)` on it with its polarity from the part, and otherwise the pair fluent and the polarity come from the main stream as in stage c2b, so that a world without location fluents reads the stream as before. A condition never reads `SAME` on a location fluent (the condition draws pass no part, and their `pair` pool is the pair fluents between entities), and a never-legal event type's redraw passes its own part as the location part too. The replacements are counted under `fix_ups.same_location_replacements`.
7. **The quota `same_rules`** (`_Dynamics.apply_same_quota`) runs after the own literals of every event type are resolved and before the fix-up rounds, so that "the rest of the precondition" exists to judge and no draw of the other streams moves. The candidates are the two-place leaves without an explicit precondition that have an able binding, lack `SAME` on a location fluent, and whose literals, the compiled literals to come, and conditions leave the literal free (`precondition_literal_fits_conditions` and `implied_conflict` on a probe for every location fluent); "some able binding can satisfy the rest of the precondition" is read as the event type having an able binding, because the other literals are achievable by construction and a precondition reads no static feature. `min(same_rules, candidates)` event types are drawn uniformly without replacement from `world:pair_preconditions:same_rules`, in label order, each with its location fluent drawn uniformly from the same part, and the positive literal is appended. The literal's slot is kept in `_Draft.quota_literal_keys`, which the fix-ups skip, and in `EventTypes.same_quota`, which the redraws of never-legal event types keep as they keep the compiled literals; `fix_ups.same_rules_added` and `same_rules_short` record what the quota did. It does nothing without location fluents or at 0.
8. **The draw order under WM.66.** The entity event types are drawn first, stage c3a's order, with the initial values, their own effects, and the effects of the event file's place entries (fixed before any draw) in their pools (`_Dynamics.extra_effects`, read by `produced`, `produced_numeric`, and `pair_adds_and_deletes`, and so by the enabling share, the achievability checks, the dead-delete rule, and `check_explicit_achievable`); the place dynamics are drawn after them with every effect of the world in theirs (the drawer's pool grows as each place event type is drawn); and the redraws of never-legal event types, `check_dynamics`, and `check_place_dynamics` read every effect. Reasons: a place event type's literals are drawn "as for the other event types", with the enabling share over the values the effects produce, which needs the entity effects first; the quota and the `SAME` literals belong to the entity draws and read no place effect; the order keeps every world without drawn place dynamics unchanged, the chain example's draws included (its explicit act's effect produces a value the entity effects already produce, so the chain world's pools and draws are those of stage c3a); and no world is made wrong, since every pool is a subset of the world's effects. Two guards for a world whose only pair fluent is a location fluent: a pair effect is drawn only with pair fluents between entities, and the `pair` pool of conditions holds them alone (without them `tiny_full` with `count: 0` crashed on an empty draw).
9. **`place_kind`** (WM.65) is written by `PlaceEventType.record` right after `kind` when the place event type is performable, read into `EventTypeRecord.place_kind`, and checked by the loader: present and `move` or `act` exactly for a place event type with a precondition or effects, agreeing with the record (`move` when an effect adds a pair of a location fluent, `act` otherwise), absent from a place event type without dynamics and from every entity event type; `PLACE_KINDS` in `places.py` names the values. The hand world writes the key for its three place event types; the check of the regenerated hand fixtures is below.
10. **The location statistics**, every key written only with location fluents: `places.place_event_types_by_kind` (`moves`, `acts`, `without_dynamics`, `explicit`), `places.dynamics` (the drawer's counts), and `places.habitats` (`habitat_stats` in `locations.py`, per location fluent: `by_place_category`, the share of each entity superordinate's entities that start at each place category, in tree order; `largest_deviation`, the share farthest from the uniform share, the category's places over the places; `entropy_by_category`, the entropy in bits of the initial places of each entity category's entities; `mean_entropy_by_level`; `max_entropy`, the uniform draw's); `pair_fluents.between_entities` and `locations` beside `count`, and `pair_fluents.place_event_types` (the place event types' literals by kind, role, and polarity, compiled literals by origin, effects by kind, and condition literals); and the episodes' `move_share`, `act_share`, and `two_place_share_entities`, which sum to `two_place_share`. `figures.py` reads them, exposes the first location fluent's habitats under the dotted key `places.habitat` (a label with a period cannot be a step of a key), and the summary line of `define` names the location fluents apart.
11. **The configurations and the off settings.** `tiny.yaml` sets `place_event_types.constraint_families: {patient: 1, cross: 1, key_lock: 1}` (WM.62), copied by `tiny_full.yaml`; `tiny_full.yaml` and `default_full.yaml` set `pair_fluents.locations: 1` (WM.63); `tiny_place_chain.yaml` sets `same_rules: 0`, so that generation still adds nothing to what its file gives (without it the quota gives two of its drawn two-place event types `SAME` on the location fluent, and its figures would move beyond WM.65), and keeps its other settings; `tiny_pairs.yaml`, `tiny_chain.yaml`, and `default.yaml` are untouched. `figures.py` gains the off setting `locations_off` (`pair_fluents: {locations: 0}`), whose baselines for the full worlds are the stage c2b worlds; `pair_dynamics_off` puts `same_rules` at 0 too, and its promise is read over the entity event types (with location fluents the place event types keep their drawn dynamics, which are location dynamics). The stage c2b tests take their forced worlds without the location fluent (`FORCED_PROFILES` sets `locations: 0`), so that they keep testing the pair fluents between entities alone, and their helpers read a place as the second argument of a location pair.
12. **The tests** (`tests/world/test_world_location_generation.py`; the fast tier on `tiny_full`, the slow tier on `default_full` and its seeds 1 to 5): the stream; the configurations (WM.62, WM.63); every place event type a move or an act with `place_kind` agreeing with its record, its location literal last with its origin, its further literals, effects, and conditions on the agent or `SOMEOF` of the place, no condition on a place's features, and both checkers over every effect (WM.66); ## Open questions

None of these changes the design; each is recorded for Jon's eye.

1. **`SOMEOF` of the place in an act** (choice 4; ruled on October 28, 2026, WM.68, below): the act's location literal entails it, so a drawn act never reads it, in a precondition or a condition; the chain example's `rest` (stage c3a) reads it as a condition, `when SOMEOF.PAIRFL.2(patient) then agent.BOOLFL.1 := 0`, which is therefore always true when the act happens (the agent is at the place). A drawn condition would be rejected as fixed by the precondition. Whether the explicit rule should refuse a condition the precondition entails is a question for the file format, not changed here.
2. **The quota's reading of "some able binding can satisfy the rest of the precondition"** (choice 7) is "the event type has an able binding": the other literals are achievable one by one, and whether a state satisfies them jointly is not decided statically anywhere in the generator. A stricter reading would need the statistics episodes, which come after the draws.
3. **`same_rules: 0` in `tiny_place_chain.yaml`** (choice 11) keeps the chain example as "what the file gives and nothing more"; without it the quota adds `SAME` on the location fluent to two drawn two-place event types of the chain world, a legitimate world, but one whose figures move beyond WM.65. The c2b test of the promise runs on the full worlds without their location fluent.
5. **A drawn place event type never reads `SAME`** on a location fluent, by stage c3a's rule that `SAME` is read between two entities; a world where a move depends on who is at the destination needs `SOMEOF` of the place, which moves draw.
6. **The habitat entropy** is reported per entity category over the places (the entropy of the category's entities' initial places), with the mean by level and the uniform draw's entropy beside it; the specification's "mean entropy of entities' initial places by entity category" could also be read as a mean over location fluents, which the default world's one location fluent makes the same.
7. **The default habitats are nearly flat (review point 2): a finding, not tuned.** (Ruled on October 28, 2026, WM.67, below.) The acceptance test (`test_the_default_habitats_are_not_flat`, the slow tier) asks that some place category's share of some entity superordinate's entities differ from the uniform share (the category's places over the 12 places) by more than three binomial standard deviations of a uniform draw of that many entities, the bound stated from the configuration. On seed 1 of `default_full` no cell does: the largest deviation is 0.119 (`PLACECATEGORY.2.1` holds 0.286 of `CATEGORY.2`'s 56 entities against a uniform 0.167), under its margin of 0.149; 3 of the 24 cells at the leaf level of the place tree lie beyond two standard deviations (about one would by chance); a chi-square over the 6 leaf place categories and the 4 entity superordinates is 30.3 on 15 degrees of freedom (significant at 5%, not at 1%), and over the 2 place superordinates 4.9 on 3; the mean entropy of the superordinates' initial places is 3.45 bits against 3.58 for a uniform draw. The cause is in the weights: `(1 + a(x, q)) ^ 1` with `a(x, q)` the number of the 4 place event types able for the binding, and `a` is 2 or 3 for 75% of the bindings (121 bindings at 0, 720 at 1, 1,137 at 2, 1,430 at 3, of 3,408), so the weights range from 1 to 4 with a mean coefficient of variation of 0.10 within an entity's row. The habitats are concentrated in the direction the capacities point, but not beyond the sampling margin of 284 entities. Following the rule that a failing bound is a finding to report and not a number to lower, the test keeps its bound and is marked an expected failure (`xfail(strict=True)`, so that it turns when the configuration or the bound changes), and the stage reports that this acceptance criterion did not hold on `default_full` seed 1. Levers for Jon, none pulled here: a higher `places.habitat_strength` (3 would spread the weights from 1 to 64), sparser place requirements (`place_event_types.constraints` weighted toward 2, or `rules` with more `AND`), or more place event types; or a joint statistic (the chi-square above) as the reading of "not flat".
