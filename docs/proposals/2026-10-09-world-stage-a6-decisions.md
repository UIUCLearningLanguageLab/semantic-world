### How the pieces stand today

The word-form pipeline is the one program that still writes the old labels: `W.<n>`, `S.<n>`, `W.<n>.S.<m>.<k>`, `F.<n>`, `AF.<n>`, `W.<n>.AF.<m>`, `M.<k>`, `W.<n>.M.<k>`, `W.<n>.M.<k>.AF.<m>`, the augmented token `W.<n>.S.<m>.<k>.A.<r>`, and the mapped token `W.<n>.S.<m>.<k>.M`. The labels are made in eight places and parsed in three (the inflect entries' words, the token number in `embeddings.py`, and `branch_of`, which reads the world's labels already). The corpus's request carries no word-form label, and `render` composes an inflected form's label from the word and affix labels it reads, so neither parses the old form. The corpus's tests and guide quote `W.12.M.2.AF.1` as the marked inflected form.

The taxonomy's configuration keys and type names are `is` and `has` (a5b choice 1), in the taxonomy's readers, outputs, and golden folders, in the world's two readers of the type names, in the corpus's concept types (`lexicon.named_proportion.is` and `.has`, the `type` column of `lexicon.csv`, and the `lexicon` block of `stats.yaml`), and in the data files, tests, and guides. The default world has 20 one-place event types and 7 two-place ones (3 superordinates of depth 2 with branching 2 then 3, pruned by the distinct-leaves requirement), and its two-place share of events is 5.7% under the defaults. `world_stats.yaml` reports no relation statistics: the constraint families, the approximate projections, the estimation flag, and the pair shortfalls live in memory, and the event types outside the density range reach the file only as warnings.

## Engineering choices made while building

Numbered for `docs/DECISIONS.md` (WM.E120 and following). Each is Claude Code's choice inside the stage's scope.

1. **One labels module.** `wordforms/labels.py` holds the prefixes and the label makers (`word_label`, `speaker_label`, `token_label`, `function_word_label`, `affix_label`, `marker_label`, `joined_label`, `augmented_label`, `mapped_label`) and the two parsers (`word_number`, for the inflect entries' `words`, and `token_number`, for the token number that `SoundEmbeddings` keys its stored tokens by). Every maker in the package calls them; `synth/__init__.py` lost `MAPPED_SUFFIX`. The labels: `WORD.<n>`, `SPEAKER.<n>`, `WORD.<n>.SPEAKER.<m>.TOKEN.<k>`, `FUNCWORD.<n>`, `AFFIX.<n>`, `WORD.<n>.AFFIX.<m>`, `MARKER.<k>`, `WORD.<n>.MARKER.<k>`, `WORD.<n>.MARKER.<k>.AFFIX.<m>`; the tokens of function words, inflected forms, and marked forms extend their labels as a word's do.
2. **Augmented and mapped tokens take readable suffixes too.** `<source token>.AUGMENTED.<recipe number>` for `.A.<recipe number>` and `<source token>.MAPPED` for `.M`. The "Labels" table lists neither; the older specification defines both. The forms are in the style of the table and need no key to read; open question 1.
3. **The reference comparison is a script, not a test.** `tests/wordforms/compare_reference_run.py` maps a run's labels back to the old forms and compares every file with a reference run: text files byte for byte, binary files by SHA-256, `config.yaml` without its provenance (the git commit differs by construction), and a front end's or embedding's `meta.yaml` without its `fingerprint`, which is a hash over the token labels and audio and so differs by construction. The reference lives outside the repository (`/Users/jon/Documents/Projects/semantic-world-reference/a6/`), so the comparison cannot run in the test suite; the acceptance check was run by hand (see below). The suite instead checks the label grammar of every CSV column of a run (`test_every_output_file_uses_the_labels_of_the_refactor` and `check_labels` in `test_wordforms_outputs.py`), and the three digest tests of the word tables (`test_content_words_are_the_stage_4_words`, `test_a_configuration_without_a_request_is_unchanged`) record the new digests beside the old ones, which the tables reproduce when the labels are mapped back.
4. **The type names are the configuration's own constants.** `FEATURE_TYPES = ("property", "part")` in `taxonomy/config.py`, with `LABEL_PREFIX`, `INPUT_TYPES`, and `FeaturesConfig.property` and `.part` following, so that the keys of `features`, `rules.input_type_weights`, and `rules.overrides`, the values of `applies_to`, the `type` column of `features.csv` and `feature_stats.csv`, the keys of `summary.yaml`'s `mean_true_features_per_instance`, and every error message carry the new names from the one place. `RENAMED_TYPES = {is: property, has: part}` drives the errors: an old key under `features`, `rules.input_type_weights`, or `rules.overrides`, or an old value of `applies_to`, fails with an error that names the new one and the stage. The similarity-bound scope `is_has` is renamed `property_part` the same way (`RENAMED_VALUES`), since it named the types. The world's `KIND_OF_TYPE` and `STATIC_TYPES` and the corpus's `CONCEPT_TYPES`, `CONCEPT_KIND`, `_KIND_OF_TYPE`, and the concepts of the lexicon use the new names; `RENAMED_CONCEPT_TYPES` of the corpus gains `is` and `has`, so `lexicon.named_proportion.is` fails naming `property`. The lexicon's named-concept draws do not change under the default proportions (a type whose every concept is named draws nothing), so the tiny corpus's content is unchanged.
5. **The golden folders keep the old type names.** `test_taxonomy_golden.py` maps the keys of the golden configurations and the `type` column of the golden frames through `RENAMED_TYPES` as it maps the labels, and the golden hashes of the five example configurations were rewritten (`features.csv`, `feature_stats.csv`, `config.yaml`, and `summary.yaml` carry the type names).
6. **The default world's tree is set in its file.** `data/world/default.yaml` sets `event_types.binary.taxonomy: {superordinates: 5, depth: 2, branching: 4}`: 20 two-place event types in 5 categories, as many as the 20 one-place ones. The code's default (3 superordinates, depth 2, branching 2 to 3) stays as the specification's "Configuration" lists it, so every other configuration and the tests' worlds are unchanged, and the file's header says that this one parameter departs from the code's default. The two-place share reached is reported below.
7. **The never-legal redraw skips an inherited literal that is no longer achievable.** The larger tree exposed a bug of a5b's `redraw_preconditions`: it rebuilt an event type's inherited literals from the feature literals, including one that the generator's fix-ups had dropped from the event type because the effect producing its value had gone, and `define` then failed the achievability check ("EVENTTYPE2.3.1: the literal NOT patient.BOOLFL.3 is not achievable" on the default world of seed 1 with 5 superordinates and 4 event types each, and on seed 1 with 4 and 5). The redraw now leaves such a literal out, as the fix-up did. `test_a_redraw_never_reintroduces_an_unachievable_inherited_literal` redraws every event type of the default world and checks the dynamics; it fails without the fix.
8. **The relations block.** `world_stats.yaml` gains `relations` (`stats.relation_stats`, built from the static side): `event_type_features`, `event_type_categories` (the non-leaf categories, as `event_types.two_place_categories` counts them), `event_types`, `constraints`, `constraint_families` (family to count), `approximate_projections` (the share of the agent and patient projections that the local test marked approximate), `proportions_estimated`, `pairs_short` (event type to the true and false pairs it had, when fewer than requested), and, when the density check is on, `outside_density` and `outside_density_labels`. The block is null in a world without two-place event types. `test_world_stats_report_the_structure` checks it on the tiny world.
9. **The notes in the older specifications** give the new forms in one paragraph each and say that the rest of the document keeps the old labels as the record of what was built. The connected-speech note gives the two utterance labels of the "Labels" table and says that the word occurrence's label takes the same style when the layer is built.

## The two-place share of the default world

With 20 two-place event types (choice 6), the share of two-place events among the events of the 1,000 statistics episodes (`episodes.two_place_share` of `world_stats.yaml`, under `uniform_event`):

| Seed | Two-place event types | Two-place share | Never legal | Precondition redraws |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (the default world) | 20 in 5 categories | 0.090 | none | 2 event types, once each |
| 2 | 20 | 0.193 | none | 3 event types, once each |
| 3 | 20 | 0.264 | `EVENTTYPE2.2.4` (never able) | 2, once each |
| 4 | 20 | 0.074 | `EVENTTYPE2.1.4` (never able) | 1, once |
| 5 | 20 | 0.083 | 1 never able, 2 after five redraws | 3 (1, 5, 5) |

The share of seed 1 was 0.057 with 7 two-place event types (a5b choice 16). Two other trees were tried on seed 1: 4 superordinates with 5 event types each gave 20 event types, a share of 0.059, and one event type still never legal after five redraws; 4 superordinates with 4 to 6 each gave 19 event types and 0.049. The share is set by the requirements, not by the count: a two-place event type's requirement holds for 0.07% to 14% of the ordered pairs of a scene's 5 participants, against one-place capacities that hold for most entities, so doubling the two-place event types adds few able two-place events. The default world's share stays under a tenth under the default policy; the balanced policy `uniform_event_type` remains the option for a study that needs half.
